Rav Huna son of Moshe bar Atzri the Priest

רב הונא בן משה בר עצרי הכהן

Letter He · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 385

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Bava Batra 174b: that his father, Moshe bar Atzri, "was the guarantor for [the ketubah of] his daughter-in-law, and Rav Huna his son was a young rabbinic scholar and was in pressing financial straits. Abaye said: Is there no one to give advice to Rav Huna that he should divorce his wife, and she should claim her ketubah from his father [and then remarry her]? — In the end, it was revealed that he was a priest [and thus could not remarry a divorcée]. Abaye said: This is what people say: Poverty follows the poor."

נזכר ב"ב קעד: שאביו משה בר עצרי ערבא דכלתיה הוה ור"ה בריה צורבא מרבנן הוה ודחיקא ליה שעתא, אמר אביי ליכא דליתביה עצה לר"ה דלגרש לאתתיה ותתבע כתובתה מאבוה — לסוף אתגלי דכהן הוא, אמר אביי היינו דאמרי אינשי בתר עניא אזל עניותא.
