R' Dostai of Bei Yesheivav

ר' דוסתאי מבי ישבב

Letter Dalet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 358

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Midrash Mishlei, end of chapter 13, as having asked R' Yehuda HaNasi: What is [the meaning of the verse] "and the belly of the wicked shall want"? And in Pesikta de-Rav Kahana 59b it reads "R' Dostai of Kokhavah," and in the note there [it states] that one must read "of Kokhava."

נזכר מדרש משלי ספ"יג ששאל לר' מאי מהו ובטן רשעים תחסר, ובפד"כ נט: גרס ר' דוסתאי מן כוכבה ובהערה שם שצ"ל מן כוכבא.
