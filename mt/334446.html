In Seder HaDorot he brings that he is mentioned in Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer chapter 55, however in Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer there are only 54 chapters, and one must read chapter 46, chapter 53 as is brought in the introduction of the Gaon Rabbi David Luria, and he wrote there that it is a scribal error and one must read R' Tanḥum, and so it is in several printed editions.

בס"הד מביא שנזכר בפדר"א פנ"ה, אבל בפדר"א אך נד' פרקים, וצ"ל פמ"ו, פנ"ג כדמובא בהקדמת הגאון רד"ל וכתב שם שהוא ט"ס וצ"ל ר' תנחום וכן הוא בכמה דפוסים.
