Rav Kahana the son of Rav Natan

רב כהנא בריה דרב נתן

Letter Kaf · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 17

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yevamot 60b, and it is implied there that he lived in the time of Rav Ashi, and it appears that one must read Rav Huna the son of Rav Natan.

נזכר יבמות ס: ומשמע שם שהיה בזמן רב אשי, ונראה שצ"ל רב הונא בריה דר"נ.
