Rava bar Taḥlifa

רבא בר תחליפא

Letter Resh · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 229

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Eruvin 40a in the time of Ravina, and this is a scribal error because Rava bar Taḥlifa was among the disciples of Rav, and without doubt it was written "R. b. A." [ר"א], which is Rav Aḥa bar Taḥlifa, and so it is in Dikdukei Soferim.

נזכר עירובין מ. בזמן רבינא, והוא ט"ס כי רבא בר תחליפא היה מתלמידי דרב, ובל"ס היה כתוב רב"א והוא רב אחא בר תחליפא וכן הוא בדק"ס.
