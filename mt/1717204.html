Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Gittin 85b, "Rav Ḥanin, and some say Rav Ḥanin Maḥozna'a, said to Rav Ashi"; however, it is a scribal error and one must read "Rav Naḥman said to him," as in Kiddushin 6b, and he is the later Rav Naḥman, as is explained in his entry.

בגיטין פה: א"ל רב חנין ואמרי לה רב חנין מחוזנאה לרב אשי, אך הוא ט"ס וצ"ל א"ל רב נחמן כקידושין ו:, והוא רב נחמן האחרון כמבואר בערכו.
