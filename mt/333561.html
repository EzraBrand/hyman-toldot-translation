Aḥiyah and Shmayah his brother

אחיה ושמעיה אחיו

They were among those killed by the government, in Ta'anit 18b. And Rashi explained that they were pious men, and he did not explain who they were. And in the Arukh, entry הרג, he explains that they are the martyrs of Lod mentioned [in] Pesaḥim 50a and Bava Batra 10b.
