Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In the Yerushalmi, at the beginning of Ma'aserot, R' Yoḥanan spoke in his name; and we have already explained under the entry for R' Simai that one must read so here as well.

בירושלמי ריש מעשרות אמר ר' יוחנן בשמו, וכבר בארנו בערך ר' סימאי שכן צ"ל גם כאן.
