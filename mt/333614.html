R' Idi of Ḥutriah

ר' אידי דחוטריה

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

A disciple of R' Yirmeyah, to whom he raised a difficulty in Yerushalmi Shekalim 2:5, Yerushalmi Beitzah 2:8, and Yerushalmi Moed Katan 1:2. He is likewise mentioned in Yerushalmi Sheviit 3:2 and Yerushalmi Shabbat end of chapter 5.

תלמידו דר' ירמיה, שהקשה לו כשקלים פ"ב ה"ה, ביצה פ"ב ה"ח, מו"ק פ"א ה"ב. וכן נזכר שביעית פ"ג ה"ב, שבת ספ"ה.
