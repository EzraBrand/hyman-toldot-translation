Aḥa bar Ḥadya, who is called Ayya Mari

אחא בר חדייא דמתקרי אייא מרי

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Gittin 35a, However, he is not one of the sages, and only someone whose name was such divorced his wife, and in the commentary of Rabbenu Ḥananel there on 34b he had the reading: Rav Aḥa bar Ḥavira, who is called Abba bar Mari.

נזכר גיטין לה., אבל הוא אינו מן החכמים, ואך אחד ששמו כן גירש את אשתו, ובפירוש ר"ח שם לד: גרס רב אחא בר חוירא דמתקרי אבא בר מרי.
