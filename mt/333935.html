Tzofnat daughter of Peniel the High Priest

צפנת בת פניאל כה"ג

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Gittin 58a it is recounted that she was taken captive, and "the captor abused her the entire night; the next day he dressed her in seven robes and took her out to sell, etc."

בגיטין נח. יסופר שנשבית ונעלל בה שבאי כל הלילה למחר הלבישה ז' חלוקים והוציאה למכור וכו'.
