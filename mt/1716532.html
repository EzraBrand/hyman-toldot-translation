Rav Sheshet of Kartiza

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned [in] Megillah 21b: "He happened to come to the house of Rav Ashi and recited the blessings represented by the acronym MNḤ [for the Torah reading]." And the reading of the Rif and Dikdukei Soferim is: "He happened to come before Rav Sheshet."

נזכר מגילה כא: איקלע לבי רב אשי ובריך מנ"ח, וגרסת הרי"ף ודק"ס איקלע לקמיה דרב ששת.
