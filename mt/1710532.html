Rav Huna bar Minyomi

רב הונא בר מניומי

Letter He · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 385

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Avodah Zarah 39a, that "he bought tekhelet [blue wool for fringes] from the household of Rav Amram the Pious. He came before Rav Yosef" (to ask him if he could trust them), "[and] he did not have [an answer] in his hand. Ḥanan the tailor met him," and he answered his question.

נזכר ע"ז לט. שזבן תכלת מאנשי דביתיה דרב עמרם חסידא אתא לקמיה דרב יוסף (לשאול לו אם יכול להאמינם) לא הוה בידיה, פגע ביה חנן חייטא והוא השיב לו על שאלתו.
