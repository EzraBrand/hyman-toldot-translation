Elazar b. Ḥanokh

אלעזר בן חנוך

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 207

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Mishnah Eduyot 5:6 disparagingly, for he treated the washing of the hands lightly, and he was excommunicated and died in excommunication, and they placed a stone upon his coffin; and as appears from there, he lived in the time of Akavya b. Mahalalel.

נזכר במשנה עדיות פ"ה מ"ו לגנאי שזילזל בנט"י ונתנדה ומת בנדוי והניחו אבן על ארונו, וכפי שנראה משם שהיה בזמן עקביה בן מהללאל.
