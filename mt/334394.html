He is mentioned in Shir HaShirim Rabbah 1, end of section 15, and there in 4:1 he said to R' Aibu. And in Bereishit Rabbah 33:6 the reading is Rav Bibi and not R' Tarya. And in Vayikra Rabbah 31:10 the reading is R' Berekhiah.
