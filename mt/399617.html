Rav Hamnuna the son of Rav Adda bar Ahavah

In Kohelet Rabbah 1:5 [it is stated] that he was born on the day his father died. And when he died, R' Avin his son was born, and it is known that the text of this statement has been corrupted.

בקה"ר פ"א־ה שנולד ביום שמת אביו. וכשמת הוא נולד ר' אבין בריה, וידוע שנשתבש הגרסא במאמר הזה.
