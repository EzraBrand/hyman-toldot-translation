In Yerushalmi Shabbat, start of chapter 1 (2b), we find: "Rav Ḥisda in the name of Ashi." However, in truth one must read: "in the name of Assi," and this is Rav Assi, who is called in the Yerushalmi almost always by this name.

בשבת רפ"א-ב: מצינו רב חסדא בשם אשי, ובאמת צ"ל בשם אסי והוא רב אסי הנקרא בירושלמי כמעט תמיד בשם זה.

Rav Aḥa the son of Rav Ashi, Zevaḥim 91a (however, there one must read: "the son of Rava").
