He was a convert, as is explicitly stated in Kiddushin 22b: "Rav Yehuda Hindua" (and Rashi explained: from the land of Kush, while the Arukh explains: from the land of Ḥavilah) "was a convert who had no heirs. He fell ill, and Mar Zutra went in to visit him, and saw that he was dying, and acquired his slave." And in Bava Batra 74b they said: "Rav Yehuda Hindua recounted: Once we were traveling in a ship and we saw a certain precious stone that was encircled by a sea-monster, etc." And see the entry for Rav Shmuel bar Yehuda.
