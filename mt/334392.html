The Tartiroi [guards/officials?] of Mahlul

טרטירוי דמהלול

Letter Tet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 107

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

Mentioned in Yerushalmi Maaser Sheni 5:2, start of the halakhah, that "he would go up and spend the Sabbath inside the Temple, and no man would rise to harvest the figs before him," and Mahlul is a place near Jerusalem, as is explained there.

נזכר מע"ש פ"ה רה"ב שהוה סליק שבית לגו בי"מהק, ולא הוה בר נש קרץ לתאינייא קדמוי, ומהלול הוא מקום סמוך לירושלים כמבואר שם.
