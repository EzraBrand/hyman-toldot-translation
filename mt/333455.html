R' Oshaia the son of Rav Idi

ר' אושעיא בריה דרב אידי

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 149

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

Thus he is mentioned in Yevamot 36b, where Rav Ashi said to him, and it is a scribal error, for one must read Rav Yehoshua the son of Rav Idi, as is brought there on 37a in Rashi.

כן נזכר יבמות לו: שא"ל רב אשי, והוא ט"ס שצ"ל רב יהושע בריה דרב אידי כדמובא שם לז. ברש"י.
