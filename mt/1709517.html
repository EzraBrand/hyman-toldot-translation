Rav Revai of Rov

רב רבאי דמן רוב

Letter Resh · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 229

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He was one of the Savoraic rabbis, and he was from the city of Rov near Nehardea, and he lived a very long life, as is brought in the Epistle of Rav Sherira Gaon, Part 2, end of Chapter 4.

הוא היה מרבנן סבוראי והיה מן עיר רוב אצל נהרדעא והאריך ימים רבים כדמובא באגרת דר"ש גאון ח"ב ספ"ד.

And he is mentioned further in the Gemara Sanhedrin 43a according to the reading of Rabbenu Ḥananel, where he said: "It stands to reason [that it comes] from the public."

ונזכר עוד בגמרא סנהדרין מג. לגרסת ר"ח, שאמר מסתברא משל צבור.
