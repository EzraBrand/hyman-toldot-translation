Savei de-Vei Rav [The Elders of the Academy of Rav]

סבי דבי רב

Letter Samekh · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 125

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

In Bava Kamma 75a, Rava said: "I have bested the Elders of the Academy of Rav," and Rashi explained that this is Rav Hamnuna, who is called thus, as in Sanhedrin 17b. However, there they said only: "'They say in the Academy of Rav' — [this is] Rav Hamnuna." And in Tosafot there [in Bava Kamma], they explain that it is Rav Huna.

בב"ק עה. אמר רבא קפחתי לסבי דבי רב ופירש"י שהוא רב המנונא שנקרא כן כסנהדרין יז:, אבל שם אמרו אך אמרי בי רב, רב המנונא, ובתוס', שם מפרשים שהוא רב הונא.
