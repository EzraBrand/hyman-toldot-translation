(Teḥinnah, I Chronicles 4:12).

(תחנה, ד"ה א' פ"ד-יב).

A wondrous story is told of him in Kohelet Rabbah 9:7, that once he was entering his town on the eve of Shabbat at nightfall with his bundle on his shoulder, and he found a man afflicted with boils lying at the crossroads. He said to him: "My master, do me an act of charity and bring me into the town." He said [to himself]: "If I leave my bundle, from where will I and my household be sustained? And if I leave the man afflicted with boils, I forfeit my life." The good inclination overcame the evil inclination, and he brought the man afflicted with boils into the town, and he went back and took his bundle and entered at sunset. Everyone was astonished and said: "Is this Abba Taḥna the Pious?" The Holy One, blessed be He, caused the sun to shine — and he was afraid lest he had received his reward [in this world]; a heavenly voice went forth and said to him: "Go, eat your bread in joy."
