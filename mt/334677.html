R' Ba

ר' בא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

Almost all of the Amoraim who are called R' Abba in the Bavli are called by the shortened form "R' Ba" in the Yerushalmi; and similarly in the Zohar, "R' Ba" is always found, and he is R' Abba.

כמעט כל האמוראים הנקראים בבבלי ר' אבא נקראים בירושלמי בקיצור לשון בשם "ר' בא" וכן בזהר נמצא תמיד ר' בא והוא ר' אבא.
