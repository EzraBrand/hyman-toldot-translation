ר' טביומי (אמורא ירושלמי)

Letter Tet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 105

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned once in Yerushalmi Berakhot 2:1 (12a), where he asked [a question] to R' Ḥizkiyah, and it is impossible to say that he is Rav Tavyomi the disciple of Abaye and Rava, for in his days the academies in Eretz Yisrael had already been destroyed.

נזכר פ"א ברכות פ"ב ה"א יב. ששאל לר' חזקיה, וא"א לומר שהיא רב טביומי תלמיד אביי ורבא שבימיו כבר נחרבו הישיבות בא"י.
