He is mentioned in Zohar Ḥadash, Ruth, Chapter 1, and it is recounted there that he fell ill, [and] R' Yosi ben Kisma and the Rabbis entered to [visit] him, and on the Shabbat day they thought that he had died, and afterward at the conclusion of Shabbat he revived, see there; and in Zohar, Shelaḥ 164b: that he would always go up to the Torah scroll [as the] sixth [reader] and he lived a long life, see there; and perhaps there were two [by this name].

נזכר בזהר חדש רות פ"א, ויסופר שם שחלש עאל לגביה ר' יוסי בן קסמא ורבנן, וביום השבת חשבו שמת, ואח"כ במוצ"ש חיה ע"ש, ובזהר שלח קסד: שהיה עולה תמיד לס"ת ששי והאריך ימים ע"ש, ועולי שנים היו.
