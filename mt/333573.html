Aḥerim [Others]

אחרים

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Horayot 13a it is recounted that because of the dispute in which R' Meir and R' Natan wanted to depose Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel from his office as Nasi, he penalized R' Meir by having him called by the name Aḥerim [Others]. However, this is not a universal rule, for we find in Sotah 12a a dispute of Aḥerim with R' Meir; and in Keritot [Sefer Keritot], Yemot Olam, he wrote that when R' Meir would state a halakhah in the name of his teacher Elisha—Aḥer—they would then call him by the name Aḥerim.

בהוריות יג. יסופר שמפני המחלוקת שרצו ר' מאיר ור"נ לדחות את רשב"ג מנשיאותו קנס את ר"מ להקרא בשם אחרים, אבל אין זה כלל כי מצינו סוטה יב. מחלוקת אחרים עם ר"מ, ובכריתות ימות עולם כתב כשהיה ר"מ אומר הלכה בשם רבו אלישע-אחר קראוהו אז בשם אחרים.

And he is mentioned by this name in Berakhot 29a, Shabbat 5a, Eruvin 58b, Sukkah 7b, 54b, Megillah 31b, Ḥagigah 4a, Yevamot 48b, Nedarim 20a, Sotah 46b, Gittin 27b, Bava Batra 96b, 107a, Sanhedrin 63a, Shevuot 35b, Avodah Zarah 30b, Horayot 9a, Zevaḥim 29a, 43b, 94a, Menaḥot 16b, 37a, Ḥullin 65a, 119a, Keritot 11a.
