In Shabbat 122b: "Shmuel happened to come to the house of Ravin Toran," and Rashi explained [that Toran is] the name of a place; and it is plausible to say that he was tall of stature and they called him after the name of a mast [toran], and like [that which is in] Bekhorot 45b.

בשבת קכב: שמואל איקלע לבי רבין תורן ופרש"י שם מקום, וקרוב לומר שהיה רם הקומה וקראוהו ע"ש תורן וכבכורות מה:.
