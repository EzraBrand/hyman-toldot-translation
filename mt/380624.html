Bar Galila

בר גלילא

In Shabbat 80b, he happened to come to Babylonia, and they said to him that he should expound on the Account of the Chariot [Ma'aseh Merkavah], and he said to them, "I will expound for you as R' Neḥemiah expounded." And this is not a personal name, but rather [means] "a Galilean" [literally, a son of the Galilee].

בשבת פ: איקלע לבבל ואמרו לו שידרוש במעשה מרכבה וא"ל אדרוש לכם כדדרש ר' נחמיה, ואין זה שם פרטי אך בן גליל.
