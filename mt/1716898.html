R' Ḥiyya bar Titas

ר' חייא בר טיטס

Letter Ḥet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 27–28

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Yerushalmi Terumot 8:3: the Rabbis of Caesarea said in his name that R' Yitzḥak bar Naḥman said in the name of R' Yehoshua ben Levi: "That which is pungent, and that which is bitter, and that which is sweet are not subject to [the prohibition of] uncovered [liquids]," and regarding this R' Ḥiyya bar Titas said: "Like that which is ground one [part] to three."

בתרומות פ"ח ה"ג רבנן דקסרין אמרין בשמו שאמר ר' יצחק בר נחמן בשם רי"בל החד והמר והמתוק אין בהם משום גלוי, וע"ז אמר ר' חייא בר טיטס כההיא דשחק חד לתלת.
