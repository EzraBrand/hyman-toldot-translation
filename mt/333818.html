In Zevaḥim 108b he asked an inquiry of R' Zeira; however, the reading of the Shitah Mekubetzet and Dikdukei Soferim is R' Avin bar Ḥiyya, and in Avodah Zarah 51b [it is] Rav Asi bar Ḥiyya.
