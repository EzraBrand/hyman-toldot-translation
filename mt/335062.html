Baha (other versions read: Batah)

בהא (ס"א באתה)

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 293

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Temurah 29a: "Baha said that R' Yoḥanan said: Until they feed it vetches of idolatry. R' Abba said to Baha: Do you dispute Ulla? He said to him: No. R' Abba said: Baha knows how to resolve a tradition, and had he not gone up there, he would not have known, for Eretz Yisrael caused [this wisdom] for him. R' Yitzḥak said to him: Baha is from here and from there" (that he studied both in Babylonia and in Eretz Yisrael, Rabbenu Nissim).

בתמורה כט. בהא א"ר יוחנן עד שיאכילוהו כרשיני עכו"ם, א"ל ר' אבא לבהא מפלגיתו אתון אעולא? א"ל לא, א"ר אבא ידע בהא לשנויי שמעתא, ואי לא דסליק התם לא הוה ידע דא"י גרמה ליה, א"ל ר' יצחק בהא מיכן ומיכן (שלמד בין בבבל בין בא"י, ר"נ).
