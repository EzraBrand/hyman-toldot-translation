R' Ḥilfai the grandson of R' Abbahu

ר' חילפיי בר בריה דר' אבהו

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Bikkurim at the beginning of chapter 2: he said, "I heard the voice of Rabbi expounding." And it is known that in the Yerushalmi all the Patriarchs are called simply by the name "Rabbi." However, it is more correct according to the version of Midrash Shmuel at the end of chapter 23: "I heard the voice of my grandfather [savi]," and out of the letter samekh [the copyist] made a resh. And in Midrash Tehillim 26:7 the version reads: R' Ḥalafta in the name of R' Aivo.

נזכר בכורים רפ"ב אמר שמעית קליה דרבי דרש, וידוע שבירושלמי כל הנשיאים נקראין בשם סתם "רבי" ויותר נכון כגרסת מדרש שמואל ספ"כג שמעית קליה "דסבי" ומסמ"ך עשה רי"ש, ובמ' תהלים פ"כו־ז גרס ר' חלפתא בש"ר אייבו.
