Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

They are mentioned in Pesaḥim 94b regarding the course of the sun, and R' Yehuda HaNasi said: "Their words appear more [correct] than our words"; and in Yerushalmi Bava Batra 8:1, that they say a son and a daughter are equally alike regarding inheritance; and [this] is brought in Shabbat 116b.
