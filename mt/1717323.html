Rav Ḥanan bar Moldah

רב חנן בר מולדה

Letter Ḥet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 54

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

[Saying] "Rav Huna said," he is mentioned in Sanhedrin 70a twice, and Rashi explained that Moldah is the name of his father. And in the old printed editions [it reads] R' Yoḥanan bar Molda, and the error came from the Soncino edition where it was printed "Rav YḤanan" and the yod had slipped there from the word "Rabi" [into the name Ḥanan], and from this it became Yoḥanan, as is brought in Dikdukei Soferim.

אר"ה נזכר סנהדרין ע. ב"פ ופירש"י מולדה שם אביו. ובדפוסים הישנים ר' יוחנן בר מולדא, והטעות בא מדפוס שונציני שנפדס שם רב יחנן והיו"ד נשמט שם מתיבת רב ומזה נעשה יוחנן, כדמובא בדק"ס.
