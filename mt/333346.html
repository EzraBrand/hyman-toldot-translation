Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Gittin 56a, [where] they objected to Rava: "Did not Avram of Bei Ḥozai teach: 'One may not collect payment,' etc.?"

נזכר גיטין נו. מקשו על רבא והתני אברם חוזאה אין נפרעין וכו'.
