ר' אבסומכוס

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 130

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Sanhedrin at the end of chapter 3, that he came for judgment with someone before R' Elazar regarding matters of a millstone and emerged liable, and after the conclusion of the judgment he brought witnesses that the law was with him, and R' Yoḥanan ruled that he could overturn the judgment.

נזכר סנהדרין ספ"ג שבא לדין עם אחד לפני ר' אלעזר על עסקי רחיים ויצא חייב ואחר גמ"ד הביא עדים שהדין עמו, ופסק ר' יוחנן שיכול לסתור הדין.
