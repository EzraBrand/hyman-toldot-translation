Rava bar Avin

רבא בר אבין

Letter Resh · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 224

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Pesaḥim 40a, and he lived in the time of Rav Naḥman and Rav Huna, and in Ketubot 23a: "R' Oshaya taught [it] on the first clause, [while] Rabbah bar Avin taught [it] on the final clause."

נזכר פסחים מ. והיה בזמן ר"נ ור"ה, ובכתובות כג. ר' אושעיא מתני ארישא, רבה בר אבין מתני אסיפא.
