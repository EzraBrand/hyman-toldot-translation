R' Ḥananiah of Kefar Tivon

ר' חנניה איש כפר טבעון

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Sifrei Ha'azinu on the verse "Were it not that their Rock had sold them", according to the reading of the Yalkut, whereas our reading is R' Yehudah of Tivim.

נזכר ספרי האזינו פ' אם לא כי צורם מכרם, לגרסת הילקוט, וגרסא שלנו ר' יהודה איש טיבעים.
