Agra

אגרא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

The father-in-law of R' Abba. He is mentioned in Ḥullin 104b with the phrasing: "Agra the father-in-law of R' Abba taught, he taught it and he explained it," and in Menaḥot 29b, that "the leg of the [letter] hei of [the word] 'ha-am' was severed by a perforation" (in his tefillin), "he came before R' Abba, etc."

חמיו דר' אבא. נזכר חולין קד: בלשון תני אגרא חמוה דר"א הוא תני לה והוא אמר לה, ובמנחות כט: שאיפסקא ליה כרעא דה"י דהעם בניקבא (בתפילין שלו) אתא לקמיה דר' אבא וכו'.
