He constantly engages in halakhic give-and-take with Rav Ashi, as in Kiddushin 21a, Bava Kamma 36a, 106a, Zevaḥim 91a, Menaḥot 6a, and he is the son of Rav Mari bar Issur who is mentioned with his brother Mar Zutra [in] Kiddushin 65b.
