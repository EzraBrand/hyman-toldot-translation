Rav Ika the son of Rav Ami

רב איקא בריה דרב אמי

He lived in the time of Rava, as in Bava Batra [original has the scribal error Bava Kamma] 149a, where he taught Isur how to transfer ownership of his money, which was deposited in the hands of Rava, to his son Rav Mari, and Rava was resentful toward him.

היה בזמן רבא כב"ק קמט. שלמד לאיסור איך להקנות כספו המונח ביד רבא לבנו רב מרי ורבא הקפיד עליו.

And he is mentioned in Bava Kamma 12a, Avodah Zarah 2a, Keritot 6b, Ḥullin 77b.
