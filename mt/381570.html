Rav Geviha of Argiza

רב גביהא מארגיזא

Letter Gimel · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 331

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Gittin 7a: Rav Huna bar Natan said to Rav Ashi: "What is the meaning of that which is written [Kinah, and Dimonah, and Adadah (Joshua 15:22)]?" - He said to him: "Rav Geviha of Argiza stated a reason for this: Anyone who has a grievance [kinah] against his fellow and remains silent [domem], He Who dwells for all eternity [shokhen adei ad] executes judgment for him." Rav Ashi said to him: "If so, what about [the verse mentioning] Ziklag [and Madmannah, and Sansannah (Joshua 15:31)]?" - He said to him: "If Rav Geviha of Bei Argiza were here, he would have stated a reason for this." And from this we see that in the time of Rav Ashi he was a renowned expounder.

בגיטין ז. א"ל ר"ה בר נתן לרב אשי מאי דכתיב - אמר לו, רב גביהא מארגיזא אמר בהא טעמא כל שיש לו קנאה על חבירו ודומם שוכן עדי עד עשה לו דין א"ל רב אשי אלא מעתה צקלג - א"ל אי הוה רב גביהא מבי ארגיזא הכא הוה אמר בה טעמא, ומזה אנו רואין שהיה בזמן רב אשי דרשן מפואר.
