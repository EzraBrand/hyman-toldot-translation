He is mentioned in Bereshit Rabbah 64:7, where he said, "'And they served her' [va'avaduha] is written," and in Vayikra Rabbah at the end of chapter 32 and Kohelet Rabbah at the beginning of chapter 4: "Daniel the Tailor interpreted the verse, etc."

נזכר ב"ר פ"סד-ז, שאמר ועבדה כתיב, ובויק"ר ספ"לב וקה"ר רפ"ד דניאל חייטא פתר קרייא וכו'.
