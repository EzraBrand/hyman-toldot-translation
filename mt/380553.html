ר' בניי

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 312

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Sanhedrin at the end of chapter 3, stating [a teaching] in the name of Shmuel and disagreeing with R' Hoshaya; and in Bereishit Rabbah at the start of chapter 8, R' Tanḥuma [reports] in his name; and in [Yerushalmi] Pesaḥim at the start of chapter 4, Ḥinena father of R' Yantah [reports] in his name.

נזכר בירושלמי סנהדרין ספ"ג אומר בשם שמואל וחולק עם ר' הושעיא, וב"ר רפ"ח ר' תנחומא בשמו, ובפסחים רפ"ד חיננא אבוה דר' ינטה בשמו.
