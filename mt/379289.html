He is mentioned at the end of Chapter 4 of Ḥallah as having brought up firstborn animals from Babylonia, and they did not accept [them] from him; and in the Mishnah of Eretz Yisrael the reading is Ben Itatam.

נזכר חלה ספ"ד שהעלה בכורות מבבל ולא קבלו ממנו, ובמתניתא דבני מערבא גרס בן אטטם.
