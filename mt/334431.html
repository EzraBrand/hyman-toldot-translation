Rav Taḥlifa the Father of Rav Huna bar Taḥlifa

רב תחליפא אבוה דר"ה בר תחליפא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yoma 6b and Keritot 25b, where he stated [teachings] in the name of Rava. Because his son's name was better known, they therefore called him by the name of his son.

נזכר יומא ו:, כריתות כה: אמר משמיה דרבא, ויען שבנו היה שמו נודע יותר לכן קראוהו ע"ש בנו.
