Ben Yehotzadak

בן יהוצדק

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 304

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Sukkah 5:4 as having been praised for his leaps (that he used to leap and dance at the Simḥat Beit HaShoevah [Rejoicing of the Place of the Water-Drawing]).

נזכר בירושלמי סוכה פ"ה ה"ד שהיה משתבח בקפיצותיו (שהיה מקפץ ומרקד בשמחת בית השואבה).
