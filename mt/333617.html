Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He had two sons who were great men of Israel and pious ones, Avuha and Minyamin, as in Megillah 28a, and they were in the time of Shmuel, as is explained in their entries; and in Eruvin 74a it reads Ivot bar Ihi, and one must read Avuha.

היו לו שני בנים גדול ישראל וחסידים, אבוה ומנימן כמגילה כח. והיו בזמן שמואל כמבואר בערכם, ובעירובין עד. גרס איבות בר איהי וצ"ל אבוה.
