Ben Dinai

בן דינאי

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Kelim 5:10: "And this is the oven of Ben Dinai." And R' Ovadiah of Bertinoro explained: "My teachers explained that it is the name of a man who used to make ovens, and I heard [that it was called so] because they debated many laws [dinim] concerning it." And there is an Eleazar ben Dinai who was a murderer; see his entry.

נזכר כלים פ"ה מ"י וזה תנורו של בן דינאי, ופי' הרע"ב רבותי פירשו שהוא שם אדם שהיה עושה תנורים, ואני שמעתי ע"ש שדנו בו דינים הרבה, ויש אלעזר בן דינאי שהיה רוצח עיין ערכו.
