Issi the father or the brother of R' Zeira

איסי אבוה או אחוה דר' זירא

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 182

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Moed Katan 20b: "And did not Issi the father of R' Zeira, and some say the brother of R' Zeira, teach in the presence of R' Zeira — R' Zeira responded after him, etc."

נזכר מו"ק כ: והתני איסי אבוה דר"ז וא"ל אחוה דר"ז קמיה דר"ז - עני ר"ז בתריה וכו'.
