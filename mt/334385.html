Tuvyah the Physician

טוביה הרופא

Letter Tet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 107

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Mishnah Rosh Hashanah 1:7, where R' Yosi recounts an incident concerning Tuvyah the Physician, "who saw the new moon in Jerusalem, he, his son, and his emancipated slave; and the priests accepted him [and his son] - and when they came to the court, they accepted him and his slave, etc." It is implied that he lived during the time of the Temple, while the court was still in Jerusalem.

נזכר ר"ה פ"א מ"ז שיספר ר' יוסי מעשה בטוביה הרופא שראה את החודש בירושלים הוא ובני ועבדו המשוחרר וקיבלו הכהנים אותו - וכשבאו לב"ד קבלו אותו ואת עבדו וכו', ומשמע שהיה בזמן הבית בעוד היות ב"ד בירושלים.
