R' Tanḥum Adriya

ר' תנחום אדרייא

Letter Tav · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 407

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Taanit 1:6, However, the statement is brought in Yerushalmi Berakhot 5:2, and there it reads R' Tanḥuma Adra'ah.

נזכר תענית פ"א ה"ו, אך המאמר מובא ברכות פ"ה ה"ב ושם איתא ר' תנחומא אדרעיה.
