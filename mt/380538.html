Ben Kufya

בן קופיא

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 310

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

In Midrash Tehillim 9:3 it is recounted that when they decreed that R' Yehudah the Baker be killed, Ben Kufya wanted to save him, and it became known to the government, and they decreed upon him as well that he be killed. And in the old Midrash Tehillim it reads by mistake "Bar Kappara," and from this [the author] erred in the book Va'ad Ḥakhamim 4a and wondered why they did not count Bar Kappara among the Martyrs of the Government.

במד' תהלים פ"ט-ג יסופר כשגזרו שיהרג ר' יהודה הנחתום רצה בן קופיא להצילו ונודע למלכות וגזרו גם עליו שיהרג, ובמד' תהלים הישן איתה בטעות בר קפרא, ומזה טעה בספר ועד חכמים ד. ותמה למה לא חשבו את בר קפרא בן הרוגי מלכות.
