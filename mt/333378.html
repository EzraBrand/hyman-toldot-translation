Rav Adda bar Rav Aza

רב אדא ברב עזא

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 136

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

Said in the name of Rav Assi, Kiddushin 81b. And it is very plausible to say that he used to come with all those who went down [from Eretz Yisrael to Babylonia] in the time of Abaye, and he is the one mentioned in Temurah 21a: "Rava bar R' Aza said: They asked in the West [Eretz Yisrael] — Abaye said to him"; and it had been written "רבא" [Rava], which is an abbreviation for Rav Adda. And Sefer Yuhasin had the reading there: "Rav Aḥa bar Rav Aza", and this is why he cited: "Rav Aḥa son of Rav Aza said in the name of Rav Abaye", and one must read: "Abaye said to him".

אמר רב אסי קידושין פא:, וקרוב מאוד לומר כי הוא היה בא עם כל הנחותי בזמן אביי והוא הנזכר תמורה כא. אמר רבא בר' עזא בעון במערבא - א"ל אביי, והיה כתוב רב"א והוא ר"ת רב אדא, והיוחסין היה גרסתו שם רב אחא ברב עזא, וזה שהביא רב אחא בריה דרב עזא אמר רב אביי וצ"ל א"ל אביי.
