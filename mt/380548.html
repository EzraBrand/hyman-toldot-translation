The People of the Galilee

בני גליל

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 311

They are mentioned in Gittin 60a, that they sent to R' Ḥelbo [asking] whether it is permitted to read from scrolls of individual books of the Pentateuch in the synagogue in public; and likewise the people of the Galilee and the people of Judea are mentioned in Eruvin 53a, that "the people of the Galilee were not careful with their language, etc., and the people of Judea were careful with their language, etc."

נזכרו גיטין ס. ששלחו לר' חלבו אם מותר לקרא בחומשים בב"הכנ בציבור, וכן נזכרו בני גליל ובני יהודה בעירובין נג. שבני גליל לא דקדקו בלשונם וכו' ובני יהודה דקדקו בלשונם וכו'.
