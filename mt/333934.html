Tzenu'in [The Modest Ones]

צנועין

Letter Tsadi · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 201

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

[They are] mentioned in Maaser Sheni chapter 5, Mishnah 1: "And the modest ones set down the coins."

נזכר מע"ש פ"ה מ"א והצנועין מניחין את המעות.

And in Yoma 39a: "And the modest ones withdraw their hands."

וביומא לט. והצנועין מושבין את ידיהם.

And in Demai chapter 6, Mishnah 6: "The modest ones of Beit Hillel used to act in accordance with Beit Shammai."

ובדמאי פ"ו מ"ו צנועי ב"מ היו נוהגין כב"ש.

And similarly we find R' Shimon ha-Tzanua [the Modest]; and see his entry.

וכן מצינו ר"ש הצנוע ועיין ערכו.
