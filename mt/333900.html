Abba, the father of R' Abba Mari

אבא אבוה דר' אבא מרי

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 41

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Berakhot 9:3, and he speaks there in the name of R' Aḥa. And in Yerushalmi Peah 1:1 (3b), it is stated erroneously "Abba, the father of R' Abba bar Mari", and one must read "Abba Mari". He was from Eretz Yisrael, approximately in the fifth generation.

נזכר ברכות פ"ט ה"ג ואמר שם בשם ר' אחא, ובפאה פ"א ה"א ג: איתא בטעות אבא אבוא דר' אבא בר מרי וצ"ל אבא מרי, ירושלמי, לערך בדור החמישי.
