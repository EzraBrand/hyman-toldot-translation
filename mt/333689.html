Elisha Ba'al Kenafayim [Master of Wings]

אלישע בעל כנפים

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 188

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

A great pious man, and concerning him it is recounted [in] Shabbat 49a that once they decreed that anyone who puts on tefillin would have his brains pierced out, and Elisha would put [them] on and go out to the marketplace. A royal officer found him and said to him, "What is that in your hand?" He said to him, "The wings of a dove," and so it was found [to be], and therefore they called him by the name Ba'al Kenafayim [Master of Wings]. And from what occurred, it appears that he was in the days of the Tannaim.

חסיד גדול ועליו יסופר שבת מט. שפ"א גזרו שכל המניח תפלין ינקרו את מוחו והיה אלישע מניח ויוצא לשוק, מצאו שוטר המלך וא"ל מה זה בידך א"ל כנפי יונה וכן נמצא, ולכן קראוהו בשם בעל כנפים, ומתוך הנעשה נראה שהיה בימי התנאים.
