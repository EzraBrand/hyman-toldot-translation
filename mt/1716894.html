In Sanhedrin 26a it is recounted that he went with R' Shimon ben Yehotzadak the second to intercalate the year in Assia. Resh Lakish met them [and] joined them. He said: "I will go [and] see how they carry out the procedure." He (Resh Lakish) saw a certain man who was plowing. He said to them: "A priest and he plows [in the Sabbatical year]?" They said to him: "He can say, etc. [that he is an imperial tax collector in the field]." When they arrived there, they went up to the upper chamber [and] removed the ladder from beneath him (so that Resh Lakish would not come up). He came before R' Yoḥanan [and] said to him: "Are people who are suspected regarding the Sabbatical year fit to intercalate a year?" Then he said: "It is not difficult for me, as it is analogous to [the case of] three cowherds"—when they came before R' Yoḥanan they said to him: "He called us cowherds and the master did not say anything to him!" He said to them: "And had he called you shepherds of sheep, what could I have said to him?"
