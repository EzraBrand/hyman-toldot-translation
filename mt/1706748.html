(Yoḥanan son of King Josiah, I Chronicles 3:15). In Nazir 65a: "As it was taught in a baraita: And how much is the measure of accumulated soil? R' Yoḥanan said in the name of Ben Azzai, etc." And the Tosafot explained that he is R' Yoḥanan ben Nuri. And without doubt the words "ben Nuri" are a scribal error, for R' Yoḥanan ben Nuri was older than Ben Azzai. And in truth, in Ketubot 8a, in Tosafot s.v. Rav, they cite simply R' Yoḥanan, who is a Tanna, from Nazir 65a. And it is likely to say that he is the one mentioned in Gittin 62a: "R' Yosi ha-Meshullam testified in the name of R' Yoḥanan his brother, who said in the name of R' Elazar Ḥisma."
