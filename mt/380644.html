בר ישיטא

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 317

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Yerushalmi Megillah 4, at the end of halakhah 10, [it is stated] that he was the speaker [amora] of R' Abbahu and the son of R' Ḥinena. "Words that he heard from his father, he would say: 'Thus said my master in the name of my father'; words that he did not hear from his father" (and R' Abbahu said in the name of his father), "he would say: 'Thus said my master in the name of R' Ḥinena.'" And see the entry for Elazar bar Yeshita.

במגילה פ"ד סה"י שהיה אמורא דר' אבהו ובנו של ר' חיננא, מילין דשמע מן אביו הוה אמר כן אמר רבי בשם אבא, מילין דלא שמע מן אביו (ור' אבהו אמר בשם אביו) הוה אמר כן אמר רבי בשם ר' חיננא. ועיין ערך אלעזר בר ישיטא.
