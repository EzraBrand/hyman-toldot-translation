R' Shila (Tanna of the Tosefta)

ר' שילא (תנא דתוספתא)

In Tosefta Kelim 4:9 Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel speaks in his name.

בתוספתא כלים פ"ד ה"ט אומר רשב"ג בשמו.

And likewise he is mentioned in Tosefta Berakhot 2:9, and it is brought in Sukkah 26a: The Rabbis taught — "In the name of R' Shila they said: A groom is exempt, etc."

ובן נזכר תוספתא ברכות פ"ב ה"ט ומובא סוכה כו .ת"ר — משום ר' שילא אמרו חתן פטור וכו'.

And in Ta'anit 11a: The Rabbis taught: "When Israel is in distress — the school of R' Shila said: Two ministering angels, etc. — they testify against him — R' Ḥidka says."

ובתענית יא .ת"ר בזמן שישראל שרויין בצער — דבי ר' שילא אמרו שני מלאכי השרת וכו' — הן מעידין עליו — ר' חידקא אומר.

And likewise he is mentioned in Mekhilta Beshallaḥ, Parashat Vayassa, chapter 1.

וכן נזכר מכילתא בשלח פ' ויסע פ"א.

And from all these places we see that he was already in the generation of Rabban Gamliel.

ומכל אלו המקומות אנו רואין שהיה עוד בדור ר"ג.

And it appears that the incident brought in Berakhot 58a with R' Shila refers to the Tanna, for they said there: "R' Shila lashed a certain man who had intercourse with an Aramean woman. He went and informed on him to the king (and in Dikdukei Soferim, in one manuscript, it reads: and informed on him to the house of Caesar), that he judges cases without the authorization of the king. They sent a royal messenger after him and asked him: For what reason did you lash the man? He said to them: Because he had intercourse with a she-donkey. Do you have witnesses? He said to them: Yes. Elijah came and testified. They said to him: If so, he is liable to the death penalty! He said to them: We, from the day we were exiled from our land, do not have the authority to execute. While they were sitting to deliberate his case, R' Shila opened and said: 'Yours, O Lord, is the greatness, etc.' They said to him: What are you saying? He said to them: Thus I am saying: Blessed is the Merciful One who gives sovereignty on earth similar to the sovereignty of the firmament, and gave you dominion, and loves justice. They said: Since the honor of the monarchy is so dear to him, they gave him a rod and appointed him as a judge (at the gate of Rome, Dikdukei Soferim). And that [man] asked him
