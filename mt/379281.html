בלווטא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Arakhin 11a, where he said [a statement] in the name of R' Yoḥanan, and Rashi explained that it is the name of a sage. And there on 28b, "Ḥizkiyah bar Biloto" — it is possible that he was his son, and it is possible that his name was Shimon, as in [Yerushalmi] Pesaḥim, start of chapter 4: "R' Shimon Bulotah in the name of R' Ḥanina."

נזכר ערכין יא. אמר בשם ר' יוחנן ופרש"י שהוא שם חכם, ושם כח: חזקיה בר בילוטו אפשר שהוא בנו, ואפשר ששמו הוא שמעון כפסחים רפ"ד ר' שמעון בולוטה בשם ר' חנינה.
