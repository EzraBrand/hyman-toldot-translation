He is mentioned in Eruvin 36b: Rav said, "The Mishnah is not [accepted as halakhah], since Ayo taught [otherwise]," and Rashi explained that it is the name of a sage.

נזכר עירובין לו: אמר רב ליתא למתני' מדתנא איו ופרש"י שם חכם.
