אבא מרי

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 89

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

Thus Rabbah b. Rav Huna was accustomed to say ["my father, my master"] when mentioning his father, who was his teacher, as in Gittin 29b, Sanhedrin 5a. And in Seder ha-Dorot he thought that it was the name of an Amora, and it is an error that proceeded from the ruler.

כן רגיל רבה בר"ה לומר כשמזכיר את אביו שהיה רבו כגיטין כט:, סנהדרין ה. ובסה"ד חשב שהוא שם אמורא ושגגה היא שיצאה מלפני השליט.
