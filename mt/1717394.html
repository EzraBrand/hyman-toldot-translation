Ḥanan the Tailor

חנן חייטא

Letter Ḥet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 55

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Avodah Zarah 39a: "Rav Huna bar Minyomi bought tekhelet [blue wool for fringes] from the members of the household of Rav Amram the Pious. He came before Rav Yosef, [who] did not have [a tradition regarding] it in his hand. Ḥanan the Tailor met him [and] said to him: Poor Yosef, from where would he have [it]? With me there was an incident where I bought tekhelet from the members of the household of Rabbanah the brother of R' Ḥiyya bar Abba, and I came before Rav Mattanah, [who] did not have [a tradition regarding] it in his hand; I came before Rav Yehudah of Hagronya (and in Rabbenu Ḥananel and in Dikdukei Soferim the word 'of Hagronya' is not present), [and] he said to me: Thus you have fallen into my hand, etc." And this is the intention of the Yuḥasin, who wrote "the chapter Ha-Tekhelet," and his intention is that he is mentioned regarding the matter of the law of tekhelet.

בע"ז לט. ר"ה בר מניומי זבן תכלת מאנשי דביתיה דרב עמרם חסידא, אתא לקמיה דרב יוסף, לא הוה בידיה, פגע ביה חנן חייטא א"ל יוסף עניא מנא ליה, בדידי הוה עובדא דזביני תכלתא מאנשי דביתיה דרבנאה אחוה דר' חייא בר אבא, ואתאי לקמיה דרב מתנה לא הוה בידיה, אתאי לקמיה דרב יהודה מהגרוניא (ובר"ח ובדקדוקי סופרים ליתא תיתב מהגרוניא) א"ל נפלת ליד וכו'. וזה כונת היוחסין שכתב פ' התכלת וכונתו שנזכר בענין דין תכלת.
