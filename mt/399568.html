R' Hillel ben Pazi

ר' הלל בן פזי

Letter He · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 406

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He was the disciple of R' Assi, as in Yerushalmi Ketubot, start of chapter 9, where he inquired of him. And similarly he is mentioned in Yerushalmi Demai 7:7.

היה תלמידו דר' אסי ככתובות רפ"ט שבעי ממנו. וכן נזכר דמאי פ"ז ה"ז.

And he had a son named R' Shimon, who inquired of him, as in Yerushalmi Sanhedrin 7:5.

והיה לו בן בשם ר' שמעון שבעי ממנו כסנהדרין פ"ז ה"ה.
