R' Taḥlifa father-in-law of R' Aḥa

ר' תחליפא חמיו דר' אחא

Letter Tav · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 405

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Berakhot 1:1 and 1:6, and both of them said in the name of R' Shmuel bar Naḥman. And the opinion of Mevo ha-Yerushalmi is that he is Rav Taḥlifa bar Maarava.

נזכר ברכות פ"א ה"א-ו ושניהם אמרו בשם ר' שמואל בר נחמן, ודעת מבוא הירושלמי שהוא רב תחליפא בר מערבא.
