Zeira bar Ḥiyya bar Ashi

זעירא בר חייא בר אשי

Letter Zayin · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 434

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Shabbat 7:2 (28a), disputing with R' Zeira. And it appears that the word "Zeira" was corrupted from the word that precedes it, as it is written there: "he is liable on account of sowing [zorea]." The word "zorea" was written twice, and afterward it became "Zeira."

נזכר שבת פ"ז ה"ב־כח. חולק עם ר' זעירא, ונראה שתיבת זעירא נשתבשה מתיבה שלפניה שכתוב שם חייב משום זורע. והיה כתוב ב' פעמים תיבת זורע ונעשה אח"כ זעירא.
