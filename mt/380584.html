Binyamin Ra'aya [the Shepherd]

בנימין רעיא

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 314

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Berakhot 40b: that he wrapped bread and said, "Blessed is the Master of this bread," [and] Rav said: "He has fulfilled his obligation." And in Yerushalmi Berakhot 6:2, "a certain Persian" asked Rav this law, and without doubt Binyamin Ra'aya was from the land of Persia.

נזכר ברכות מ: שכרך ריפתא ואמר בריך מריה דהאי פיתא אמר רב יצא, ובירושלמי ברכות פ"ו ה"ב חד פרסוי שאל לרב הדין הזה, ובל"ס שבנימין רעיא היה מארץ פרס.
