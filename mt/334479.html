R' Tanḥum of Parod

ר' תנחום דמן פרוד

Letter Tav · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 410

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

In Avodah Zarah 31a it is recounted that "R' Yoḥanan happened to come to Parod. He said: 'Is there any mishnah of Bar Kappara?' R' Tanḥum of Parod taught him [a baraita]," and it is implied there that he was a disciple of Bar Kappara.

בע"ז לא. יסופר שר' יוחנן איקלע לפרוד אמר כלום יש משנת בר קפרא תנא ליה ר' תנחום דמן פרוד, ומשמע שם שהיה תלמידו דבר קפרא.
