(A Tanna of the Baraita).

(תנא דברייתא).

He is mentioned in Shabbat 106b: regarding the law of one who traps locusts on Shabbat, he says, "If they were streaming and coming, he is exempt," and so it is in the Tosefta there, chapter 13 [perhaps 12]. And he is also mentioned in Yevamot 102a [perhaps 120a] and Bava Metzia 27a: that one may testify [to identify a corpse] on the basis of a mole.

נזכר שבת קו: בדין הצד חגבים בשבת אומר אם היו מקלחות ובאות פטור, וכן הוא בתוספתא שם פ"יג, וכן נזכר יבמות קב. וב"מ כז. שמעידין על השומא.
