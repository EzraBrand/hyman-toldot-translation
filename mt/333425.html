In Yevamot 10a he stated before Rav Kahana in the name of Rava, and in Sanhedrin 92a he raised a difficulty to R' Elazar and R' Elazar answered him; and a scribal error occurred there and one must read Rav Aḥa Qaraḥna'ah, as in Diqduqei Soferim, and so it is in Berakhot 33a.
