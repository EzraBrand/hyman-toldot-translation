Ikhu (name of a woman)

איכו (שם אשה)

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 173

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

She is mentioned [in] Taanit 25a, that she was the neighbor of R' Ḥanina b. Dosa, and she complained before him that she was building a house and the beams were too short. He asked her what her name was, [and] she said to him: "Ikhu." He said to her: "Ikhu, may your beams lengthen."

נזכרה תענית כה. שהיתה שכנתו של ר' חנינא בן דוסא והתאוננה לפניו כי תבנה בית והכשורות קצרות ושאלה מה שמה א"ל איכו. אמר לה איכו נממי כשוריך.
