Avimi bar Tovi

אבימי בר טובי

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 119

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He was the disciple of R' Abbahu, as in the Yerushalmi Nazir, start of chapter 9, where a certain gentile woman came before R' Abbahu, [and] he said to Avimi bar Tovi: "Go out and open [with a ground for absolving her vow] by means of an anticipated event [nolad]," and in Yerushalmi Bava Batra 2:3: "R' Avimi bar Tovi ruled."

היה תלמידו דר' אבהו כירושלמי נזכר ר"פט שנכרית אחת אתאי לקמיה דר' אבהו א"ל לאבימי בר טובי פוק ופתח לה בגולד, וב"ב פ"ב ה"ג הורי ר' אבימי ב"ט.
