Rav Sama

רב סמא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Menaḥot 42a it is recounted that "Ravina and Rav Sama were sitting before Rav Ashi; Rav Sama saw the cloak of Ravina that had frayed [and the fringes had been drawn closer to the corner] - he said to Ravina: 'Does the master not hold [that this is disqualified as "you shall make, and not from that which is already made"]?' - he said to him: 'That was stated regarding the time of making.' Rav Sama was embarrassed; Rav Ashi said to him: 'Do not be distressed, for one of them is like two of us'" (and Ravina was from Eretz Yisrael, Rashi). And the Gaon, the author of Seder HaDorot, Part 2, page 474, was very right that one must read "Rav Abba and Rav Sama the son of Rava," and this Rav Abba was indeed a resident of Eretz Yisrael, as is explained in his entry.

במנחות מב. יסופר שרבינא ורב סמא הוו יתבי קמיה דר' אשי, חזייה רב סמא לגלימיה דרבינא דסתר - א"ל לרבינא לא סבר לה מר - א"ל בשעת עשייה איתמר, איכסיף רב סמא א"ל רב אשי לא תתקיף לך, חד מינייהו כתרי מינן (ורבינא מא"י היה, רש"י), וצדק מאוד הגאון בעדה"ד ח"ב צד 474 שצ"ל רב אבא ורב סמא בריה דרבא, וזה רב אבא היה באמת בר א"י כמבואר בערכו.

And he is mentioned in Bava Metzia 10a after Ravina.

ונזכר ב"מ י. אחר רבינא.
