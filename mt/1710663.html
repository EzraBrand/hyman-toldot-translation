R' Zeira the son of R' Abbahu

ר' זעירא בריה דר' אבהו

Letter Zayin · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 434

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Berakhot 8:6, where they said: "One does not recite a blessing over the lamp until one benefits from its light; R' Zeira the son of R' Abbahu expounded: 'And God saw that it was good,' and afterward, 'and He divided.'" And in Bereshit Rabbah 3:6, that he expounded in Caesarea; and in Yerushalmi Berakhot 9:1, he spoke in the name of his father R' Abbahu; and in Yerushalmi Avodah Zarah, chapter 3, end of halakhah 1, the words "R' Abbahu" are missing.

נזכר ברכות פ"ח ה"ו שאמרו אין מברכין על הנר עד שיאותו לאורו דרש ר' זעירא בד"ר אבהו וירא אלהים כי טוב ואח"כ ויבדל. ובב"ר פ"ג־ו שדרש בקיסרין, וברכות פ"ט ה"א אמר בשם אביו ר' אבהו, ובע"ז פ"ג סה"א חסר תיבת ר' אבהו.
