R' Yehudah the Baker

ר' יהודה הנחתום

He was in the generation of R' Akiva, as is proven from Yerushalmi Rosh Hashanah 1:5: R' Yehudah the Baker said (and in Rosh Hashanah 22a it states simply R' Yehudah): "Heaven forbid! R' Akiva was not excommunicated; rather, it was the head of Gader, and Rabban Gamliel sent and removed him from his headship." And in Bava Batra 132a: R' Yehudah the Baker said, "There was an incident involving my sister's daughter, a bride, etc." (and Rashbam explained that his son married his sister's daughter). And similarly he is mentioned in Menaḥot 6b: R' Yosi ben Yosi ben Yasyan and R' Yehudah the Baker said, "In what case are these words said? When he took the handful, etc." And in Yerushalmi Ḥagigah, start of chapter 2, and Kohelet Rabbah 7:8, [it is stated] that he was among the martyrs of the government, and Elisha ben Abuyah saw his tongue placed in the mouth of a dog, [and] he said, "This is Torah, and this is its reward!"

הוא היה בדור ר"ע כדמוכח ר"ה פ"א‬ ה"ה א"ר יהודה הנחתום (ובר"ה כב. ‬איתא‬ סתם ר' יהודה) חו"ש לא נחנדה ר"ע‬ אלא ראש גדר היה ושלח ר"ג והעבירו‬ מראשיתו. וב"ב קלב. אר"י הנחתום‬ ‫מעשה בבת אחותי כלה וכו' (ופי' הרשב"ם שבנו נשא את בת אחותו), וכן נזכר במנחות‬ ו: ר' יוסי בן יוסי בן יאסיין ור' יהודה‬ הנחתום אמרו במד"א שקמץ וכו'‪.‬‬ ובירושלמי חגיגה רפ"ב וקה"ר פ"ז-ח‬ שהיה מהרוגי מלכות, ואלישע בן אבוי‬ ראה לשונו נתון בפי הכלב, אמר זו‬ תורה וזו שכרהה.

And similarly he is mentioned in Eikhah Rabbah chapter 2, on the verse "The Lord has swallowed up" [Lamentations 2:2], and Midrash Tehillim 9:13, that Ben Kufya wanted to save him, and it was decreed upon him as well that he should be killed.

וכן נזכר באיכ"ר פ"ב פ' בלע ומד'‬ תהלים פ"ט-יג שבן קופיא רצה להצילו‬ ונגזר גם עליו שיהרג.
