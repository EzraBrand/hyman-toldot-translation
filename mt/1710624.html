Zigud

זיגוד

Letter Zayin · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 417

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

Regarding him the well-known proverb was said: "Tuviah sinned and Zigud is lashed," as in Pesaḥim 113b and Makkot 11a, because he came before Rav Papa and testified alone against Tuviah that he had sinned, and [Rav Papa] lashed Zigud.

עליו נאמר המשל הנודע טוביא חטא וזיגוד מנגיד כפסחים קיג: ומכות יא. כי בא לפני ר"פ ואסהיד לחודיה על טוביא שחטא ואנגיד לזיגוד.
