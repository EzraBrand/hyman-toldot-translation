The Elders of Nezunya

סבי דנזוניא

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

In Kiddushin 25a, they did not come to the public lecture of Rav Ḥisda. He said to Rav Hamnuna, "Go and place them under a ban" — [and in the end] they said to him, "Not Hamnuna [related to the word for fish], but Karnuna [related to the word for cold]!" [playing on his name after he embarrassed them with a halakhic question].

בקידושין כה. שלא אתו לפיקריה דרב חסדא א"ל לרב המנונא זיל צנעינהו - אמרו ליה לאו המנונא אלא קרנונא.
