R' Abba Kif'

ר' אבא כיפ'

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 88

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Sukkah 3:10 before R' Yonah, but the Korban HaEdah explains that R' Abba said "bend your hand" [kof yadkha], while the Penei Moshe explains it as R' Abba of Kifah.

נזכר ירושלמי סוכה פ"ג ה"י קומי ר' יונה, אבל הקרבן העדה מפרש שר' אבא אמר כוף ירך, ובפני משה מפרש ר' אבא דמן כיפה.
