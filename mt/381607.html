Gidal bar Re'ulai

גידל בר רעולאי

Letter Gimel · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 335

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

In Gittin 34a, [it is recorded] that he sent a bill of divorce to his wife, and he lived in the time of Abaye and Rava. And there, 58b, [it is recorded] that he "received land from the residents of a valley in exchange for paying its land tax, [and] he came before Rav Papa." And it is not known whether he was a rabbinic scholar.

בגיטין לד. ששלח גט לאשתו והיה בזמן אביי ורבא, ושם נח: שקיבל ארעא בטסקא מבני באגא - אתא לקמיה דר"פ, ואין ידוע אם היה צורבא מרבנן.
