It was taught: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel [one must read: R' Shimon ben Menasya] says: 'These seven qualities that the Sages enumerated in the righteous were in R' Yehuda HaNasi.' Who is R' Yehuda HaNasi? He is R' Yehuda HaNasi. R' Abbahu said: 'He is R' Yehuda HaNasi, he is R' Yudan HaNasi, he is Rabbenu.'"

מנסיא אומר הנוי והכח וכו' לצדיקים נאה להם ונאה לעולם, תני ר״ש בן גמליאל [צ״ל בן מנסיא] אומר אלו שבע מדות שמנו חכמים בצדיקים היו ברבי, מאן רבי הוא ר' יהודה הנשיא, א״ר אבהו הוא רבי הוא ר׳ יודן הנשיא הוא רבינו.

And in Dorot ha-Rishonim, part 2, page 56, he already wondered about this and wanted to emend it as a corruption there. However, it seems simple, for it is known that R' Shimon ben Menasya was a colleague of R' Yehuda HaNasi and it is possible that he was older than him, and he only saw R' Yehuda HaNasi reach old age and hoary head, whereas regarding his sons, he did not see whether they lived long like their father; for in truth, his son Rabban Gamliel did not live long, and also his honor was not as great as the honor of R' Yehuda HaNasi, as he did not fill his place in Torah. Therefore, R' Shimon ben Menasya stated the phrasing in a general way: "These seven qualities were all fulfilled in R' Yehuda HaNasi and his sons," meaning: all of them together possessed the seven qualities. However, R' Yoḥanan, who saw with his own eyes R' Yehuda HaNasi and his son Rabban Gamliel—who did not reach the level of his fathers, neither in years nor in wisdom—and in his days R' Yehuda Nesiah was also the great man of the generation, therefore he said, "All seven qualities were (only) in R' Yehuda HaNasi"; and so that we should not err that his intention was regarding the other Patriarchs, all of whom are called by the name "Rabbi," he therefore explains: "Who is Rabbi? He is R' Yehuda HaNasi." And even though R' Yehuda Nesiah was also the great man of the generation, it is possible that when he said this, R' Yehuda Nesiah had not yet reached the years of hoary age. However, R' Abbahu, who was the disciple of R' Yoḥanan and merited further to know R' Yehuda Nesiah and his greatness and his glory, therefore came and added: "He is Rabbi, he is R' Yudan HaNasi, he is Rabbenu"—that in him too all the seven qualities were fulfilled.
