R' Avin bar R' Elazar

ר' אבין בר ר' אלעזר

Letter Alef · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 124

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon), not reviewed. Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions. Check every reading against the Hebrew.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Sanhedrin 2:4, where he said regarding the matter of Saul: "It was a fence within a fence"; however, it is a scribal error, and one must read as the statement is brought in Midrash Ruth, end of chapter 4: "R' Avin in the name of R' Elazar."

נזכר בירושלמי סנהדרין פ"ב ה"ד שאמר בענין שאול גדר לפנים מגדר היה, אך הוא ט"ס וצ"ל כדמובא המאמר במ"ר ספ"ד ר' אבין בשם ר' אלעזר.
