R' Bezira

ר' בזירה

Letter Bet · All entries · Hebrew on Wikisource · scan p. 294

Machine translation (Gemini 4 Argon). Hyman's own (parentheses) are kept; [gray brackets] are the translator's additions.

He is mentioned in Yerushalmi Berakhot 8:5, [where] R' Levi [speaks] in his name, but this is a scribal error and one must read "in the name of R' Nezira," as in the Amsterdam printing; and so too the statement is brought in Bereishit Rabbah 12:6 and the end of chapter 82: "R' Levi in the name of R' Nezira."

נזכר בירושלמי ברכות פ"ח ה"ה ר' לוי בשמו, אך הוא ט"ס וצ"ל בשם ר' נזירה כבדפוס אמשטרדאם וכן מובא המאמר ב"ר פי"ב-ו וסוף פפ"ב ר' לוי בשם ר' נזירא.
