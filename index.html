Aaron Hyman, Toldot Tannaim ve-Amoraim

אהרן הימן, תולדות תנאים ואמוראים (לונדון, תר״ע / 1910)

Complete (20-Nov-2026). Every entry of Aaron Hyman's biographical dictionary of the Tannaim and Amoraim that is transcribed on Hebrew Wikisource, 1,889 entries, has an English machine translation next to the Hebrew. Wikisource has no entries yet for the letters Mem, Ayin and Pe, and only a few for Yod; they will be added when they are transcribed. Two entries also have a reviewed translation with a summary at the top and each quoted source set out with the Hebrew first.

Machine translation of all entries

All entries in English machine translation: all 1,889 entries that are transcribed on Wikisource (about 285,000 Hebrew words), translated paragraph by paragraph with Gemini 4 Argon and shown next to the Hebrew, with the length of each entry in the index. The model was chosen in a test of seven models on two entries (cost, speed and accuracy); see pipeline/README.md. A free check compares every number in the English with the Hebrew. Paragraphs whose Wikisource text is uncorrected OCR are marked. Corrections are welcome as GitHub issues.

Reviewed entries

  • R' Abba (stam) Letter Alef. Hyman argues that there were only three sages called simply "R' Abba," not seven, and corrects a dozen scribal errors.
  • R' Yoḥanan Letter Yod. Origins, youth and teachers of R' Yoḥanan bar Nappaḥa.
  • Text in (parentheses) is Hyman's own. Text in [square brackets], shown in gray, is the translator's addition.
  • Summaries, section headings and tables are the translator's.
